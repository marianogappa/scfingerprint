Add two sets of replays: for example a player's known games, and games you suspect someone else played. We'll say whether both sets were played by the same person, and check each set against the catalog.

How reliable is this?
  • It needs at least different 1v1 games per player. One game is too little: plenty of people look like a pro in a single game.
  • It only names a player when the evidence is strong. A near miss is reported as inconclusive, with no name attached.
  • "Not in the catalog" means the player isn't one of the catalogued players. It says nothing about how good they are.
  • Ladder maps and the player's main race work best. Team games and very short games are ignored.
  • Treat a result as strong evidence, not proof. Confirm by hand before accusing anyone.
